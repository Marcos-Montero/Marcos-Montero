# Marcos Montero

### Serious systems. A playful mind.

I build products and the engineering teams behind them. Financial platforms, games, music tools and unusual interfaces: different ways of turning complex problems into software people can use.

**Founding Engineer & Head of Engineering at Fynza. Previously CTO at Enso Coliving.**

[Explore my website](https://marcosmontero.com) · [How I build & lead](https://marcosmontero.com/experience) · [Selected projects](https://marcosmontero.com/projects) · [Let’s talk](https://marcosmontero.com/contact)

## Building with responsibility

At **Fynza**, I started development as the sole engineer in April 2026, building the financial and risk-evaluation platform from its first technical decisions. We launched the MVP for the tourism sector in Mexico in early September. As of September 2026, our engineering team has grown to eight. International expansion is planned for 2027.

Before that, as **CTO at Enso Coliving**, I worked across technology strategy and a connected PropTech platform, bringing booking, payments and verification into one experience.

The work that interests me combines product judgment, hands-on engineering and helping a team deliver. I’m interested in conversations about **CTO, Head of Engineering and Founding Engineer** opportunities.

[Fynza case study](https://marcosmontero.com/experience/fynza) · [Enso Coliving case study](https://marcosmontero.com/experience/enso-coliving)
